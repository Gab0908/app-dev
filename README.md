# My Favorite Series and Movies 🎞️
by Gabriel Feliciano

---

### Movies:
- **Insidious Movies**
  - *Overview:* A supernatural horror franchise following a family haunted by sinister entities from a dark realm called *The Further*.
  - *Why I liked it:* The creepy environment, creative jump scares, and the lore behind astral projection keep me interested.
  
- **Hunger Games**
  - *Overview:* A dystopian sci-fi series where teenagers from oppressed districts are forced to fight to the death in a televised arena.
  - *Why I liked it:* I liked the build-up of the story and the intense, strong lead character played by Jennifer Lawrence, and themes of rebellion against a dystopian government.

- **Kill Bill**
  - *Overview:* A stylized action-thriller about a former assassin seeking vengeance against her ex-colleagues after a brutal betrayal.
  - *Why I liked it:* The fight choreography makes it cool and fun to watch, an iconic soundtrack, and a satisfying plot.

---

### Series:
- **Stranger Things**
  - *Overview:* A sci-fi horror series set in the 1980s where a group of kids uncovers secret government experiments and a parallel dimension called the *Upside Down*.
  - *Why I liked it:* The old-school vibes, story build-up, and the mix of unknown and monster-horror.
    
- **American Horror Story**
  - *Overview:* A horror anthology series where each season tells a completely different, self-contained story with a recurring cast.
  - *Why I liked it:* Every season feels new with unique themes, great plots, and memorable characters.
    
- **Fate Series**
  - *Overview:* An action-fantasy anime franchise centered around the *Holy Grail War*, where mages summon historical and mythical heroes to fight for a wish-granting relic.
  - *Why I liked it:* The top-tier/peak animation keeps me watching, epic fight scenes, and the use of historical figures as heroic spirits to fight alongside masters.
